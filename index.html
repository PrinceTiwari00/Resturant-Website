The shop

A Chinese street-food counter on JP Marg

Kungfu Noodles — 江湖面馆 — is a tight, busy room just off Thamel's main drag, set up to look like the street stalls of southern China. One wall is covered from end to end with photographs of every dish, each one numbered and priced. That wall is the menu, and it is what this site was copied from.

The kitchen cooks a narrow, specific thing: noodles, handmade, in Sichuan and Hunan preparations — Chongqing, Wuhan, Beijing, Yibin — with a cold counter of offal cuts and vinegared vegetables alongside. Reviews keep coming back to the same two points: the heat is real, and a bowl is enough for two.

It is a plain room with wooden stools and a fridge of cold bottles. People come back for the noodles and for the fact that the price has barely moved.
