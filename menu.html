Nothing matches that

Try “noodle”, “buff”, “dumpling” — or clear the filters.

Prices are in Nepali Rupees and include taxes. Dishes marked “Chicken or Buff” come with your choice of protein. Please tell us about any allergies before you order — our kitchen uses peanuts, sesame, wheat gluten, egg and shellfish, and everything is cooked in the same wok.

Spice is measured in flames: one is gently warm, three is a serious Sichuan hit. We can always add more chilli oil, never less pain.

Menu prices are current as of November 2026. The wall menu in the shop is always the final word — if you spot a difference, the wall wins.
