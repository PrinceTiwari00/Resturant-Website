Nothing matches that

Try “noodle”, “buff”, “peanuts” — or clear the filters.

Prices are in Nepali Rupees and are the ones printed on the board. Tell the kitchen about any allergy before you order — peanuts, sesame, egg and wheat noodles appear all over the board, and a small kitchen cooks all of it in one place.

Spice is measured in flames, copied from the flames printed beside each dish on the board: one is gently warm, three is the hottest thing on the wall. Ask for less.

Where these prices come from. They are read off the two picture boards in the shop, one price at a time — nothing here is estimated. The boards list more numbers than we could photograph clearly, so a few dishes are left off rather than guessed at. The wall always wins.

Menu prices are current as of November 2026. The wall menu in the shop is always the final word — if you spot a difference, the wall wins.
