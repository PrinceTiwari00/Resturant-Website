How an order runs

From tap to table, in four steps

  1. 01
    Choose your bowls. Tap + beside anything on the menu. Your order is kept on this device, so you can add to it later.
  2. 02
    Send it. Name, phone, pickup or delivery — then WhatsApp opens with the whole order written out.
  3. 03
    We confirm. If a dish has sold out or you've asked for something unusual, we call you back before cooking.
  4. 04
    Pay on hand-over. Cash, eSewa or Fonepay at the counter or at your door. No prepayment online.
Ask a question

Good to know

How long does takeaway take?

15–20 minutes at lunch, a little longer after 7 PM when the shop fills up. Call ahead and we'll have it bagged.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum for pickup. Our own delivery inside Thamel starts at Rs 1,000; courier apps set their own thresholds.

Can you make it less spicy?

Yes. We'll pull back the chilli oil, but Sichuan peppercorn is the soul of the dish — expect some tingle.

Do you cater for groups?

We do. For eight or more bowls, message us a day ahead on WhatsApp and we'll plan the timing.

Allergies?

Tell us before ordering. The kitchen works with peanuts, sesame, wheat gluten, egg and shellfish in the same wok.
