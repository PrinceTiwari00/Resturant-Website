Getting here

Find the red lanterns on JP Marg

From Thamel chowk, walk north up JP Marg for about two minutes, past the trekking shops. We're the narrow shopfront with red lanterns and a wall of food photographs.

Landmark

Opposite the Kathmandu Guest House lane, next to a small handicraft shop. Look up for the lanterns.

Best times

Quietest between 2 and 5 PM. Busiest after 7 PM — call ahead if you're a group of six or more.

By taxi or bike

Ask for “Kungfu Noodles, JP Marg” — drivers in Thamel know it. Two-wheelers can pull up to the door.

Before you ask

Frequently asked

Do you take table reservations?

We're a small shop, so we don't hold tables. For eight or more, message us on WhatsApp a day ahead and we'll plan timing and seating together.

Is there anything vegetarian?

Plenty: tomato & egg noodles, vegetable noodle soup, cold noodles, smashed cucumber, black mushroom salad, vegetable dumplings, spring rolls and more. Filter the menu by “Vegetarian”.

Can I order in advance for a flight or a trek?

Yes — call us about 30 minutes before you need it and we'll time the cooking so it's hot when you arrive.

Do you accept cards?

We take cash, eSewa and Fonepay QR. Cards aren't reliably accepted in Thamel, so bring cash as a backup.

Is the food halal?

We cook with buff and pork and do not operate a halal kitchen. Please ask us directly if you have specific requirements.

Do you deliver outside Thamel?

Our own delivery covers Thamel, Chhetrapati, Durbar Marg, Lazimpat, Paknajol and Sorhakhutte. Beyond that, use Foodmandu or Pathao Food.
