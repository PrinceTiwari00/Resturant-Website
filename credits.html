Photo & font credits

Who made the parts of this site that aren't noodles.

Photography

All food and interior photography on this site is AI-generated imagery created for this project as placeholder art direction. It illustrates the style of the dishes and the room — it is not a literal record of a specific plate served at the shop. Before launch, replace it with the restaurant's own photographs; the image pipeline in scripts/build-assets.mjs will resize and recompress them automatically.

Typefaces

  • Fraunces — Undercase Type. SIL Open Font License 1.1. Self-hosted subset in assets/fonts/.
  • Manrope — Mikhail Sharanda. SIL Open Font License 1.1. Self-hosted subset in assets/fonts/.

The full license text ships alongside the fonts in assets/fonts/OFL.txt.

Build & content

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript — no framework, no tracking scripts, no cookie banner. The menu is generated from assets/data/menu.json; images are processed with ImageMagick. Google Maps loads only after you tap “Load the map”.

Business details and review quotations are drawn from the restaurant's public listings. Review text is quoted for attribution and edited only for length.
